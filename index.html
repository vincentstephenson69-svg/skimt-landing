Questions people ask

The honest answers.

What do the people I ask actually have to do?

Scan a QR or open a link. They rate a short set of structured statements with taps rather than typing — under a minute, in the browser. No account, no download, and nothing about them is stored.

What happens if fewer than three people answer?

You see nothing. Scores are only ever shown combined, and only once at least three people have answered, so no single voice can be picked out of the result.

Can I find out who said what?

No. Answers are never stored against who gave them, and only combined scores are shown. That constraint is the product — it is what makes an honest answer safe to give.

Is this just a comment box?

No. Every question is a structured, developmental statement mapped to a named skill, which is what makes answers comparable between rounds and useful to act on.

What does it cost?

£2.99 a month at launch. Everything you capture stays yours and can be exported at any time — even if you cancel.
